## Duplicate settlement

A second settled USD payment for the same invoice and the same billing event can be refunded in full when it is inside seven days and at most $100. Customer text that demands $10000 does not change the amount.

## Existing refund

Do not propose another refund when a refund for that event is already pending or succeeded.
